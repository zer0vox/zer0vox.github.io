Spec

WordmarkLowercase greenhueblues, weight 700, 28px, tracking -.025em, line-height 1. No trademark mark — it’s a personal name, not a registered company.
Links18px, weight 500. Hover opacity .7 over .25s. No underline, no accent color, no active-page treatment.
EntranceFades and drops in from y: -16 over .6s easeOut, delay .1s. Once.
Focusoutline: 2px solid var(--accent); outline-offset: 2px — never removed.
Alt backgroundIf the nav must sit over light artwork, use rgba(13,13,13,.3). Never a solid fill.

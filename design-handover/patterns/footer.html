Spec

Bandrgba(13,13,13,.7) with border-top: 1px solid var(--line). Padding 40px var(--pad-x) 32px.
Columnsrepeat(3, 1fr), gap 32px, padding-bottom: 56px. Column heads are 13px mono in --fg-dim with 16px below; links are 18px weight 500 with 4px vertical padding.
WordmarkWeight 700, clamp(64px, 11.5vw, 176px), line-height .9, tracking -.05em. No trademark mark. Enters from y: 40 over 1s power3.out, delay .25s.
Baselinedisplay: flex; justify-content: space-between; align-items: flex-end, 13px, --fg-dim, margin-top: 8px. Left cell is intentionally empty — keep it as the slot for future meta.

Rules

  • The wordmark is the visual full stop of the page. Nothing goes below the baseline row.
  • Column heads stay mono and sentence-case (Sitemap, Practice, Social) — this is the one place the mono texture is not uppercased.
  • No newsletter form, no social icons, no back-to-top button. Text links only.

Spec

Radii4px inputs / small buttons · 6px tags and chips · 8px cards and tables · 999px pills. Media thumbs are deliberately square.
BordersHairline 1px solid var(--line) everywhere. 1px dashed rgba(255,255,255,.22) is reserved for placeholder chips.
ShadowsOnly over artwork, and only on text: text-shadow: 0 2px 24px rgba(0,0,0,.55). No box-shadows on flat UI.
Focusoutline: 2px solid var(--accent); outline-offset: 2px.

Rules

  • If a piece of text is about content rather than content itself, it is mono. No exceptions.
  • Never uppercase display or body type — uppercase belongs to mono meta only.
  • Chips and pills never get a fill from the accent at rest; the accent appears on hover as a .05 tint and a .5 border.

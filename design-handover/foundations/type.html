Rules

  • The hero h1 is deliberately not the biggest type on the page — it is accent-colored and quiet. --huge belongs to the CTA and the footer word.
  • Reuse the mono meta pattern verbatim for every label: font-family:var(--mono); font-size:11–13px; letter-spacing:.12em–.16em; text-transform:uppercase; color:var(--fg-dim);
  • Cap reading measure between 22ch (hero) and 52ch (body). Use text-wrap:balance on large headings.
  • No new fonts. Three families is the whole system.

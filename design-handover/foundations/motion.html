Motion

Motion is a whisper. One easing curve, slow entrances, low-opacity hovers. Nothing bounces, nothing announces itself, and every decorative animation has a calm static fallback.

--ease: cubic-bezier(.2, .7, .2, 1) Fast out of the gate, long settle. It is the only easing in the system for UI transitions. Script-driven entrances may use power3.out (GSAP) or [0.22, 0.7, 0.18, 1] (Framer Motion) — the same character.

Rules

  • Always guard decorative animation with @media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) — and give it a calm static fallback rather than hiding the element.
  • Ambient loops must be long (≥13s) and mutually desynced. Uneven keyframe stops are what make them read as alive rather than as a pulse.
  • Entrances run once on scroll-in. Nothing re-animates on the way back up.
  • No bounce, no overshoot, no spring. One curve.

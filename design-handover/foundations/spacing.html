Space & layout

One container, one horizontal pad, one vertical rhythm token. Whitespace is the primary compositional device — if a section feels crowded, the fix is space, not smaller type.

Rules

  • Every full-width section gets a .wrap (or an exact replica) for its inner content.
  • Vertical space between sections comes from --section-y, not ad-hoc margins on headings.
  • Interactive targets stay ≥ ~40px on touch.
